Student QR Generator

Copy any number of columns from your spreadsheet and paste them below. The generator ignores parent details, email addresses, contact numbers, and unrelated columns. It uses only the student name and the exact 12-digit LRN from the same spreadsheet row.

For printing, use Print / Save vector PDF. QR shapes and names stay sharp when enlarged. Print the PDF directly, rather than a screenshot of it.

Each page is exported at 5656 × 8000 pixels. Up to 35 students fit on one page; additional pages are downloaded together in a ZIP.
